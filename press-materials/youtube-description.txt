Mini Me-Mao is a cozy desktop companion that lives right on your screen. Drag your little dollhouse anywhere you like, then let your mini unpack, paint, and decorate while you work, study, or play.

🎨 AN ARTIST WHO WORKS WHILE YOU DO
Your mini paints on their own in the background. Check in and see the pieces of art that they've created, then sell them to the gallery for in-game gold.

🛒 NEW STOCK TWICE A DAY
Browse a shop that rotates with deals twice a day across furniture, decor, apparel, and more. Cozy up your dollhouse with over 150 charming decorations.

👗 DRESS UP YOUR MINI
Give your mini a look of their own, with over 135 trillion possible character combinations. Unlock new styles as they become available in the apparel shop, and give your mini ways to level up their skills such as fitness equipment or books to read.

🖼 PAINTING STYLES TO UNLOCK
Level up your mini's painting skills to unlock new ways for them to paint, from simple Doodles to intricate Master works, each one improving in quality as your mini gets better. Master each painting in the collection!

FEATURES
• A desktop companion you can place anywhere and have run in the background
• Sell finished paintings for gold in the gallery
• Shop daily deals and decorate with furniture, wallpaper, flooring, and knick-knacks in your room
• Dress up your mini with a wide array of outfits
• Idle painting: your mini creates art while you work or play on other tasks
• Unlockable painting styles, each improving in quality as your mini's skill grows
• Resize the game to fit your screen how you like
• Minimize the game to a non-intrusive docked version
• No popups, no timers, no pressure: every menu is player-driven

Available now in early access for Windows, macOS, and Linux, with a browser demo if you'd like to try it first. Coming to Steam — page announcement soon.
